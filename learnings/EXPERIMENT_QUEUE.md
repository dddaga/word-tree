  ~~**HYPOTHESIS — student > teacher.**~~ **RESOLVED NO by `vlm_step006` (p=0.813, n=3859).** The reasoning that flagged it as unsettled was correct: +5.5pp = 11 net images at n=200, and `same` 0.705 means 59 prediction-discordant images, so McNemar's b−c = 11 with b+c ≤ 59 admitted p from ~0.001 (11/0) to ~0.19 (35/24) — the aggregate JSON could not settle it. At n=3859 it lands at +0.2pp, CI [−1.3, +1.7]pp. **The correct claim is parity at −16.58% params and 1.60×** — still the best lever in the trajectory.

  What survives the correction: **no label ever touched the student** (only supervision = teacher post-connector features under relative MSE; class labels enter `train_student` nowhere), and at n=3859 `agree` = 0.499 — it disagrees with the teacher on half the images at identical accuracy. So feature distillation does **not** converge the student onto the teacher's decision function. What dies: the "smoothed teacher / denoiser" explanation, refuted by the balanced discordance b=440 vs c=432.

  Still open and now the top question: this was unlabelled **in-domain** adaptation, so does the *parity* survive off-domain? Decides whether the drone must collect target-domain frames before deploying, or can distil on anything → `vlm_step007`. Clean discriminator = distil on 5 wnids, eval on the held-out 5 (CIFAR is resolution-confounded).

  **CONFIRMED — logit-KD is a monotone LIABILITY here, and it does not fix the fidelity caveat it was built for.** top-1 falls monotonically in β: 0.560 → 0.420 → 0.280. `agree` never beats the β=0 baseline at any weight (0.420 vs 0.280 / 0.380 / 0.220). The dose-response is the strong part of the evidence: with β=0.1 also losing, this is **not** a mis-tuned mixing weight that a sweep could rescue — the KD gradient and the feature gradient genuinely conflict, and even a 10% dose costs 14pp of accuracy while degrading feature fit 57% (rel_mse 0.2573 → 0.4029). Decision rule (top-1 ≥ 0.560 AND `agree` > 0.420) failed on both counts by every arm. **Feature-MSE alone ships. The `agree` < top-1 gap goes into the paper as a stated limitation of feature distillation, not a solved problem.**

  **What KD actually buys, stated precisely.** Only eval-KL, the metric nearest its own training objective: pure KD is the best arm on KL (2.161) while being the **worst** on `agree` (0.220). Soft-distribution match ≠ argmax identity. *(Supersedes the reading first written here from the β=1 arm alone — "logit-KD buys functional fidelity" was FALSE, drawn before the isolation arm existed.)*

  **Best side-finding, from the α=0 arm — worth a paper sentence.** Pure KD ends at rel_mse **29.1**, cosine 0.4985, having fallen only 11% over 10 epochs: the features drift essentially unconstrained, retaining almost no geometric relationship to the teacher's. Yet the frozen text model still reads **0.300 top-1 out of them (3× chance)**. **The readout tolerates near-arbitrary feature geometry provided the output distribution is steered** — it is not matching a learned code, it is decoding whatever is presented. This is the same "readout is permissive" theme as step605 / det_step002, arriving from the opposite direction.

  **Design (as run).** Targets vlm_step004's caveat — `same` 0.660 / `agree` 0.420 below top-1 0.560 means feature-MSE does not preserve the teacher's function pointwise. step605's actual recipe distilled soft **outputs**, not intermediate features, so restore that term: `loss = α·relMSE(post-connector features) + β·KL(student‖teacher)` on the LM's next-token distribution at the answer position — the only place the whole pipeline's function is observable. `--beta 0` reproduces vlm_step004 exactly and `--alpha 0` isolates pure logit-KD, so the two terms can be separated rather than assumed. Script `scripts/frontier/vlm_step005_logit_kd.py` (200 lines, uses `vlm_eval.py`). The KD path is one image per forward (the target lives at one token position), so gradient accumulation `--accum 8` restores vlm_step004's effective batch — a bs=1 update drove cosine to −0.03 in the first smoke. Teacher caches both features and logits once (~74 KB + ~98 KB per image, fp16 CPU). **Smoke test PASSED** (cpu, 40 img, 6 ep): rel_mse 11.04→1.08, KL 8.11→4.05, and kd_d6 already reached top-1 0.300 vs naive 0.100 with KL-vs-teacher 11.651→4.377. Incidental finding: naive-init rel_mse is ~11–15, which is why vlm_step004's ep1 average of 0.61 was a fast early drop, not a starting point. **Metric that decides it:** `agree`, not top-1 — top-1 on a closed 10-way task can be right for the wrong reason, which is the whole caveat.
