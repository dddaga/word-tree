## 5. Reframes

1. **What if the soft routing β-anneal mechanism IS the new default routing, not a side experiment?** step859 T0 already shows +1.22pp over hard gather with NO AH, NO ΔW. The compound (step859 + AH + ΔW) could be the new paper efficiency baseline.

2. **What if K=1 distillation at N=4096 gives a better operating point than K=5 N=2048?** N=4096 teacher=97.30%; K=1 student inherits a stronger teacher and might hit 96%+ at the same 34K param count.

3. **What if writing the paper NOW is the single highest-priority experiment we could run?** The claims are proven. Every additional experiment is either (a) incremental confirmation or (b) new mechanism not in the paper. Neither unblocks submission.

4. **What if the BFS frontier size (step855) is telling us something about information density per input — that most neurons are "computation waste" per inference?** If C_quiet_zero survives (quiet nodes add nothing), SGNNET already does adaptive computation without knowing it, and the paper can claim this.

5. **What if D=8 gives ≥95% at 2× fewer position parameters, improving the params story?** Current params: W_pos [2058, 16] = 32,928; at D=8 = 16,464. Down from 34,976 to ~18K total params. Better headline number.

6. **What if the soft routing gradient through W_pos distances is why D_very and ΔW-proj work — all three mechanisms co-adapt W_pos geometry?** They share the W_pos gradient path. The compounding rule says orthogonal mechanisms are safe — but if they're all W_pos-path, we need an isolation ablation at T1.

7. **What if the paper's intro claim should be "topology-native computation" rather than "efficient FC"?** Current framing: SGNNET is a smaller FC replacement. Alternative: SGNNET is a network that learns its own computation topology — routing emerges from W_pos geometry. This positions soft routing as the canonical mechanism, not a follow-up.
