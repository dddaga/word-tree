1. **Attack 1: Claim 2’s “<1% params matches VGG16 FC” conflates *total* FLOPs with *FC-head-only* FLOPs, masking that SGNNET’s routing loop dominates compute despite low param count.**  
   Why it matters: The paper claims “parameter efficiency” as a headline advantage, but if total wall-time or FLOPs (including conv feature extraction) are worse than VGG16, the efficiency claim collapses for end-to-end inference.  
   Minimal experiment to falsify: Run end-to-end inference (VGG16 conv + head) on 10k Imagenette samples for both models and report wall-time, true FLOPs (including conv), and accuracy.  
   Severity: BLOCKER

2. **Attack 2: Claim 1’s “random sparse graphs are effective” ignores that K_in=25 (out of 25088) is *not* a Johnson–Lindenstrauss embedding — the input dimensionality (25088) >> target neuron capacity (D=64), violating JL’s requirement d ≥ O(log N / ε²).**  
   Why it matters: The theoretical justification collapses if random projections *do not* preserve distances — implying performance gains may stem from overfitting or data leakage rather than geometry preservation.  
   Minimal experiment to falsify: Replace K_in=25 with a true JL projection (e.g., Gaussian matrix to d=1000) + same SGNNET routing; compare accuracy. If drop >5pp, JL claim is invalid.  
   Severity: BLOCKER

3. **Attack 3: Claim 7 (compounding interference) uses N=1024 to infer diminishing returns at N=4096, but no ablation tests *why* interference occurs — e.g., whether it stems from overparameterization, gradient conflict, or routing saturation.**  
   Why it matters: If interference is due to experimental artifact (e.g., insufficient training epochs for compound config), the “routing ceiling” hypothesis is unfounded and misleads architectural design choices.  
   Minimal experiment to falsify: Train step131-C compound config with 2× epochs and report Δ vs single mechanisms; if gain recovers, interference is not structural.  
   Severity: MAJOR

4. **Attack 4: Claim 6’s scale transfer compression assumes mechanisms are *additively* beneficial at lower N, but no control for baseline accuracy differences — e.g., W_proj’s +5.48pp at N=1024 may reflect overfitting to a smaller dataset, not transferable signal.**  
   Why it matters: If higher N=4096 is *inherently harder* (e.g., due to fixed feature noise), apparent compression may reflect dataset difficulty, not architectural ceiling.  
   Minimal experiment to falsify: Train same mechanisms on *downsampled* Imagenette (e.g., 5k images) at N=1024; if Δ vanishes, compression is dataset-size artifact.  
   Severity: MAJOR

5. **Attack 5: Claim 8’s novelty assertion omits *routing-by-repulsion* in self-organizing maps (SOMs, Kohonen 1986) and neural gas (Kohonen & Maravall 1997), which use repulsive neighbor updates on feature space to form topology-preserving maps.**  
   Why it matters: If SGNNET’s anti-Hebbian ΔW signal is functionally equivalent to neural gas dynamics (even without message passing), the “novel routing primitive” claim is overstated.  
   Minimal experiment to falsify: Implement neural gas on VGG features (same D, K_hh=4) with same training protocol; compare accuracy and routing signal geometry.  
   Severity: MAJOR

6. **Attack 6: Claim 4’s “mean-pool readout is load-bearing” ignores that attention readout failure (step118) may stem from *overfitting* due to extra parameters, not routing geometry — attention heads add ~10× params vs mean-pool.**  
   Why it matters: If attention fails due to param budget mismatch, the conclusion that “simple aggregation is best” conflates architectural prior with regularization effect.  
   Minimal experiment to falsify: Train attention readout *with weight decay* or reduced hidden dimension to match mean-pool param count; if accuracy recovers >90%, the load-bearing claim is spurious.  
   Severity: MAJOR

7. **Attack 7: Claim 3’s “learning happens in routing dynamics, not weights” is undermined by step886 showing θ thresholds are *neutral* — yet θ is the only learned parameter in some ablations, implying learning may occur elsewhere (e.g., W_pos).**  
   Why it matters: If θ is non-essential, the paper’s core thesis (“routing dynamics encode knowledge”) lacks a clear learning substrate — W_pos could be doing the heavy lifting, but no ablation isolates it.  
   Minimal experiment to falsify: Freeze W_pos and train only θ + fc_out; if accuracy drops >10pp, routing dynamics depend on W_pos, not θ.  
   Severity: MINOR

8. **Attack 8: The GLNN claim (Claim 7) uses T=2 as optimal, but no baseline compares SGNNET teacher to a *hard-label* student trained on VGG16 logits (same T=2).**  
   Why it matters: If hard-label student matches SGNNET teacher, the “routing geometry” interpretation collapses — soft targets may not encode unique structure.  
   Minimal experiment to falsify: Train MLP_37 on hard labels from VGG16 (no SGNNET) at T=2; if accuracy ≥97.81%, GLNN gain is illusory.  
   Severity: MINOR
